# Clean catalog layout — v151

The catalog uses a black canvas, a compact centered header, portrait posters, quieter type and consistent spacing. Poster titles and dates sit below the artwork. Save, Like and Details controls are separate from the playback link.

The homepage includes trending, popular and top rated movies and series, followed by new movie releases. The hero displays the full title and actual TMDB rating and date. Search opens from the header; mobile navigation remains available at the bottom.

The welcome page uses the same design, with a short introduction and three preview rows. Browse, details, account, profiles and appearance settings share the neutral palette.

Assets: `public/clean/clean.css` and `public/clean/clean.js`.
